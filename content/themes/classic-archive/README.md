# Classic Archive

A bundled Lumora Fiction theme in the style of a traditional fandom archive: a navy header, a parchment page and compact, boxed story blurbs that are quick to scan down a long list.

## Using it

Open **Appearance > Themes**, find Classic Archive and press **Use this theme**. Storybook stays the default until you do.

## What readers get

- Story blurbs with a square for the rating and one for the completion status, the title and author, the fandoms in bold, a list of tags and a one-line summary of words, chapters and last update. Every square also carries its meaning as text for screen readers.
- A Search page with the search form and its filters in a side column (above the results on a phone).
- Light and dark colours. Readers press the colour button in the header to step through Auto (match their device), Dark and Light, or set it on their account page.
- Text size and reading width controls above every chapter, remembered in the browser and saved to the account of a signed-in reader.
- Skip link, keyboard-friendly navigation and a print layout that leaves out everything but the story.

## Theme options

Readers can switch on a quiet **Reader mode** for a chapter (with the button in the reading controls or the R key), and open a story with several chapters as one page. The site's footer text, set under **Appearance > Theme options**, is printed in the footer.

**Appearance > Theme options** offers the story text typeface (leave it on "use the theme's own" for the theme's serif), whether the footer shows the "Powered by" credit, and **Show a picture band under the site header**. The band is off until you turn it on; when it is on, choose Dusk, Ink or Meadow, a picture you added yourself, or your own upload. The picture is decorative and is not printed.

- **Upload your own picture** on the same screen takes a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture. A wide, short one (around 1600 by 300 pixels) suits the band, because it is cropped to fill it. After saving, choose **Uploaded picture** in the list. **Remove this picture** deletes it.
- **Add pictures by FTP.** Put a file named `banner-something.jpg` (also `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` or `.svg`, in lower case) in `content/uploads/theme-headers/` and it appears in the list, so `banner-sea-glass.jpg` is "Sea glass". Keep a picture under 3 MB (an SVG under 256 KB). A file that is not really that kind of picture, or an SVG that contains script or links to other files, is not listed. Theme updates never touch this folder.
