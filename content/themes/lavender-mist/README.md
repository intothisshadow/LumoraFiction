# Lavender Mist

A bundled Lumora Fiction theme in soft teal, lilac and iris on warm cream, with gradients on the header, footer, buttons, headings and cards. Chapters, pages and news posts are each drawn as one rounded card, and the story text sits on a near-flat surface so it stays easy to read.

## Using it

Open **Appearance > Themes**, find Lavender Mist and press **Use this theme**. Storybook stays the default until you do.

## What readers get

- Light and dark colours. Readers choose between matching their device, light or dark from the colour menu in the header, or from their account page.
- Text size and reading width controls above every chapter, remembered in the browser and saved to the account of a signed-in reader.
- Styled dropdown menus. In browsers that cannot style their own list, a small script in the theme draws a matching one that works with the keyboard and screen readers. With scripts off, the ordinary menu shows.

## Theme options

**Appearance > Theme options** offers the story text typeface (leave it on "use the theme's own" for the theme's serif) and whether the footer shows the "Powered by" credit.
