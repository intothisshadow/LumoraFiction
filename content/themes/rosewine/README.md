# Rosewine

A bundled Lumora Fiction theme in deep wine, dusty rose and blush on warm cream, with gradients on the header, footer, buttons, headings and cards. Chapters, pages and news posts are each drawn as one rounded card, and the story text sits on a near-flat surface so it stays easy to read.

## Using it

Open **Appearance > Themes**, find Rosewine and press **Use this theme**. Storybook stays the default until you do.

## What readers get

- Light and dark colours. Readers press the colour button in the header to step through Auto (match their device), Dark and Light, or set it on their account page.
- Text size and reading width controls above every chapter, remembered in the browser and saved to the account of a signed-in reader.
- Styled dropdown menus. In browsers that cannot style their own list, a small script in the theme draws a matching one that works with the keyboard and screen readers. With scripts off, the ordinary menu shows.

## Theme options

Readers can switch on a quiet **Reader mode** for a chapter (with the button in the reading controls or the R key), and open a story with several chapters as one page. The site's footer text, set under **Appearance > Theme options**, is printed in the footer.

**Appearance > Theme options** offers the story text typeface (leave it on "use the theme's own" for the theme's serif) and whether the footer shows the "Powered by" credit.
