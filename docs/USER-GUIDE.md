# Lumora Fiction user guide

This guide is for the people who use a Lumora Fiction site: readers browsing and searching, and writers publishing stories. Site administrators should also read the [administration guide](ADMINISTRATION.md).

## Accounts

Visitors have their own pages at `/account/sign-in` and `/account/register`, linked from the site's main menu. Anyone with an account, whatever their role, can sign in there.

- **Registering.** The form asks for a private login name, a public display name, an email address and a password. If the site asks for email confirmation, the new person is told to check their inbox, and the account starts working when they open the link. The page gives the same answer whether or not the address already had an account, so it cannot be used to find out who is registered. If registration is closed, the page says so and the menu link disappears.
- **Signing in and out.** Sign in with the login name or the email address. After five wrong passwords from one address, sign-in is paused for fifteen minutes, and the page says how long to wait. A person who has not yet confirmed their address is offered a new confirmation email. **Sign out** is a button on the account page.
- **Forgotten passwords.** **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page emails a link that works once and expires after an hour. Opening the link does not use it up; only choosing a new password does. The page says nothing about whether the address has an account.
- **My account.** Signed-in people open **My account** in the menu to change their display name, email address and password. Changing the email address or the password needs the current password, and the old address is told about an email change. Changing the password signs the person out everywhere else. Authors also choose here how the chapter editor opens for them, or keep the site default; the choice is hidden while the administrator locks everyone to one mode.
- **Writer links.** Authors, Editors and Administrators find **Post New Story** and **My stories** on their **My account** page, and Editors and Administrators also find **Awaiting approval**. Readers see none of them.
- **Moving between the site and the admin.** While you are signed in as an Author, Editor or Administrator, the site's main menu shows an **Admin** link, and the admin header has a **View site** link back to the home page. Readers and visitors never see the Admin link.
- **Light and dark.** The admin area follows your device's setting, and the button in the header switches it by hand.

## Reading the archive

The home page lists the most recently published and the most recently updated stories, with the latest news in a column beside them (below them on a phone). Each story has a page at `/story/42-its-title` with its cover, summary, notes, details, tags and chapter list, and each chapter is read at `/story/42-its-title/1`, with the author's notes, previous and next links, and its place in the story. A finished story that has a single chapter shows its text on the story page itself. When you are signed in and may change a story (its author, an Editor or an Administrator), each chapter in its list also has an **Edit** link that opens the chapter in the admin; no one else sees it. Every author who has published something has a page at `/author/7-their-name`, and every fandom, character, relationship, genre, tag, category, rating, warning and relationship category has its own listing, for example `/fandom/good-omens` or `/tag/slow-burn`. Lists can be sorted by recent update, publication date, title or length, and are split into pages of twenty stories.

**News** is in the main menu, at `/news`, while news is switched on. It lists the published announcements ten to a page, pinned posts first and then the newest, and each post has its own page showing its title, date, the author's display name and the text. The latest few also appear at the top of the home page. News is open to everyone who can reach the site, including when the archive is set to require sign-in, so it can tell people about downtime or a rule change.

**Browse** is in the main menu, at `/browse`. It links three plain lists, **New stories**, **Recently updated stories** and **Completed stories**, and an index for each kind of classification that has something to show: fandoms, genres, categories, tags, ratings, archive warnings and relationship categories. An index lists each name with the number of stories that use it, A to Z or most used first, with a row of first letters (and `#` for names that start with a digit or a symbol) to jump to one, and is split into pages of a hundred. Only names an administrator has reviewed, and that at least one public story uses, are listed, so a new tag proposed by a writer or a tag that exists only on a draft does not appear until it qualifies (its own page still works). A kind that the archive mode switches off is not offered. Characters and relationships have no index of their own, because there are too many; a fandom's page links to its most used characters and relationships instead.

**Search** is in the main menu, at `/search`. Type words to find stories whose title, summary, author name or tags contain all of them, and open **Filters and sorting** to narrow the results by rating, archive warning, genre, category, fandom, tag, whether the story is ongoing or finished, its language, and a range of word counts; the kinds the archive mode switches off are not offered. Fandoms and tags are typed by name (an alias works too); the others are picked from a list. Results can be ordered by recent update, publication date, title or length. Every filter in use is shown above the results, and clicking it removes it. The page works without scripts, and its addresses can be bookmarked or shared. A search handles up to six words of 100 characters and shows at most the first thousand matches, split into pages of twenty, so a very broad search is better narrowed with a filter. Search pages are kept out of search engines.

Only stories that are published, and chapters that are published, are ever shown. A draft, a story waiting for approval, an unpublished story or a deleted one gives the same "page not found" as an address that never existed, so nobody can tell it is there; its author and the staff who can edit it are taken to the private preview instead. A listing for a tag or fandom with no public stories, and the page of an account that has published nothing, also answer "not found". If the administrator turns on **Visitors must sign in to read**, anyone who is not signed in is sent to the sign-in page instead of seeing any story page.

## Bookmarks, continuing and reading preferences

Anyone signed in can press **Bookmark this story** on a story page, or **Bookmark this chapter** on a chapter page, and open **My bookmarks** from the **My account** page to see the list and remove a bookmark. The site also remembers the chapter you opened last in each story and how far down it you got, so the story page offers **Continue reading**, a chapter offers **Continue where you left off**, and **Continue reading** on your account page lists every story you have started, marks the finished ones, and lets you forget one or clear the list. **Follow this story** on a story page (or the tick box when bookmarking) adds it to the stories the site watches for you, and **Updated stories** on your account page lists the ones with a new or revised chapter since you last read them; with **Tell me when a story I'm reading has been updated** switched on in the Reading section, the story page, the account page and the Continue reading page also say so. Following is always your choice, and **Stop tracking** or **Stop tracking all** ends it without touching your bookmarks or places. Nothing is recorded for visitors who are not signed in. A story that its author later takes down disappears from your bookmarks without any message, and returns if it is published again. The **Reading** section of your account page sets colours, text size and reading width, and these follow you to any device you sign in on; the Storybook theme's own controls on a chapter save to your account as well. Bookmarks, places and preferences are private to your account and are deleted with the stories they point to. Each account can keep up to 500 bookmarks and follow up to 500 stories.

## Writing and publishing

- **Stories.** Where Authors write. **My stories** lists your own stories, searchable and filterable by status; Editors and Administrators also get **All stories**. Click a column heading (**Title**, **Author**, **Status**, **Chapters**, **Words** or **Last changed**) to sort the list, and click it again to reverse it; the arrow shows the column in use, and the search and status filter are kept. Tick several stories to publish, unpublish or delete them together; stories that are not yours, or that still lack the classification the administrator requires, are skipped and counted. **Add a story** opens the story form: title, summary, notes, disclaimer, language, where the story stands, an optional cover picture, and the classification lists the archive setting allows (a list marked "needed before publishing" must be filled in first). Fandoms, characters, relationships and tags are typed as a comma-separated list, and the entries that already exist and match what you are typing appear under the box: choose one with the arrow keys and Enter (or by clicking it) to use its exact spelling. An entry found through an alias is entered under its proper name, and one no administrator has reviewed yet is marked as new. Characters and relationships are narrowed to the fandoms you have already named. Without scripts the boxes still work as plain text.
- **Covers.** A cover is a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture of up to 2 MB (less if your host allows less), with a short description for readers who cannot see it; after you choose a picture, a frame in the shape the story page uses appears over it, and you can drag it or use the sliders to crop the picture (or leave it alone and use the whole picture); the site then resizes it and saves it again, so details in the file such as where a photo was taken are not kept. Covers need PHP's GD extension. Replacing or removing a cover, or deleting the story, removes the old picture files.
- **Publishing.** Open a story to publish it, send it for approval, unpublish it, preview it, or manage its chapters. A story waits as a draft, and an Author's first story may wait for approval, depending on the site's settings; after one of their stories has been published, later ones publish directly. If a reviewer sends a story back, it returns to a draft with the reason shown on the story form and a **Not approved** mark in the list, until you send it for approval again.
- **Chapters.** A chapter has a title, optional notes, and the editor with a live word count and separate **Save draft** and **Publish** buttons; **Move up** and **Move down** change the chapter order. A chapter is only visible to readers once both it and its story are published. **Preview** shows a draft the way it will read, to you and to staff only. Leaving a form with unsaved changes asks first, and if your session ends while you write, the page hands your text back so you can sign in again in another tab and carry on without losing it. Deleting asks on a page of its own and cannot be undone.
- **Author profile.** Authors, Editors and Administrators can tell readers about themselves. **About you** takes a short text, up to 10,000 characters, written in the same editors as a chapter (Visual, Markdown, HTML or plain text) with the same formatting, and **Picture** takes a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture (at least 100 pixels wide and tall, up to 2 MB, with the same optional crop as a story cover) that is resized to a 100 by 100 square. Both appear in an **About the author** section above the **Stories by** heading on your author page, and nothing is drawn until you fill something in. Everything here is public. The screen is also linked as **Author profile** under **Quick links** on your account page. Administrators can see both on a person's page under **Users** and use **Clear author information** or **Remove picture** (recorded in the activity log) if something should not be public.
- **Notifications.** Authors, Editors and Administrators have an inbox, under **Stories** and as **Notifications (N)** in the header when something is unread, for what needs them about stories. Reviewers are told when a story is waiting for review, and a writer is told when a reviewer approves their story, sends it back (the reason itself is on the story's form) or unpublishes it. Each message links to the page that deals with it, can be marked as read, and is marked as read by itself once the thing it asks for has happened. Nobody is told about what they did themselves. It is separate from **Alerts**, which are about the site and only for Administrators.
- **Trash.** **Move to the trash** on a story's page, on a chapter's page or from the bulk menu takes it out of every list and away from readers without deleting it. A story in the trash is unpublished; a chapter in the trash leaves the story, and the chapters after it move up. **Stories > Trash** lists what you have trashed (Editors and Administrators can also show everyone's), where you can **Restore** it, as a draft or unpublished and never straight to readers (a chapter comes back at the end of its story), or **Delete for good** after a confirmation page. What stays in the trash is deleted for good after the number of days the administrator has set. **Delete the story** and **Delete this chapter** still delete at once. Bookmarks and reading places that pointed at a trashed chapter are dropped.
- **Downloads.** When the site allows it, readers can take a published story away as an EPUB (for e-readers and reading apps), a PDF or a single HTML page, from the **Download** button on the story page. The file holds a title page (title, your display name, summary, rating, warnings and tags) and every published chapter in order; your sign-in name never appears in it or in its file name. Your default for all your stories, including ones you publish later, is the **Downloads** box on your **Author profile**. For one story, the **Downloads** choice on its screen can use your default, allow downloads, or refuse them, and that choice wins over your default in both directions: you can refuse everything but allow one story, or allow everything but refuse one. Drafts, stories waiting for approval and unpublished stories can never be downloaded. A PDF is only made for stories up to 100,000 words; longer ones are offered as EPUB or HTML.
- **Copying a story.** **Make a copy** on a story's page creates a new draft with the same details and classification and every chapter as a draft. It has no cover picture, and none of the readers' bookmarks. A copy is yours, even if you copied another writer's story as an Editor or Administrator.
- **Earlier versions of a chapter.** Each time a change replaces a chapter's title, text or notes, the version it had is kept. **Earlier versions** on the chapter's page lists them with who saved them, shows any of them as it reads, and **Go back to this version** puts it back; what it replaces is kept too, so going back can be undone. Only the newest versions are kept; the administrator sets how many.
